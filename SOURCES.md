# Public framework sources

The app's organization and exam-emphasis assumptions should be periodically checked against current official sources.

- National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians — Paramedic examination/test-plan materials: https://www.nremt.org/
- National EMS Education Standards / EMS.gov: https://www.ems.gov/
- American Heart Association — 2025 CPR & ECC Guidelines and algorithms: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines
- Public product description/table-of-contents information for *Nancy Caroline's Emergency Care in the Streets, 9e* from Public Safety Group/Jones & Bartlett Learning.

This project deliberately avoids copying proprietary textbook prose, illustrations, or test-bank questions.
